`build_cure_cycles.py`, where every cycle is typed in its source's own units
(°F, psi, inHg or °C, bar) next to the line it came from. 30 cycles:

## Assumptions (flagged on every affected step)

- **Ramp not stated** (most MIL-HDBK-17 summaries, M18/1 alternative, HexTool):
  1-3 °C/min, the range most of these datasheets state.
- **Cooling not stated**: 2-3 °C/min.
- **8552 US ramp to 350 °F**: the 3-5 °F/min of step 2 (the datasheet does not
  restate it).
- **Easy Composites 80 °C cycle**: pre-heated oven, modelled as a 10 °C/min entry.

Every assumption appears in the planner's CHECKS list as "Assumption: …".
